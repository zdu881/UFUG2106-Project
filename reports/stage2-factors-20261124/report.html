Sharing Boolean functions across outputs

UFUG2106 · Stage 2 · Group 2 · Zeyun DU (50025854) and Jincan Li (50032637) · 24 November 2026

From a truth table to a circuit

The submitted Stage 1 method mapped Shannon decompositions to AND2, OR2 and NOT gates using several input orders. Stage 2 compares alternative output cones on a shared gate graph and rewrites small cuts. We added a way to discover a reusable internal signal directly from the truth tables. A shared signal costs area once; its arrival time matters wherever another output uses it.

For an output F(B,R), candidate input groups B come from the overlap between two output supports. Fix each assignment to B and read the resulting truth table over the remaining inputs R. If exactly two distinct tables occur, a bit h(B) identifies which one to use. Therefore F(B,R) = G(h(B),R) on every assignment. We recurse on h and G, construct small residual functions directly, and reuse identical gates. Candidate circuits compete on excess delay, then total weighted area, then delay. An output outside the search can retain its original cone.

What changed on a four-bit adder?

The incoming circuit used area 961 at delay 20. Factoring produced area 144 at delay 23, within the target of 25. The generating circuit used area 152 at delay 25. Exhaustive signal matching on all 4,096 assignments identified all three internal carry bits in the candidate, each used three times. The solver receives only the truth table and gate costs/limits.

Both programs passed all 18 official tests and all 18 targets, with exhaustive independent replay. These are three designs with six variants each.

Cost of the optional pass

Public cases: in a separate instrumented run the pass changed 2 of 50 circuits and saved 5 total area units. On 48 unchanged circuits the median pass time was 5.5 μs and the maximum 3.700 ms. Independent timed portfolio runs can produce different circuits; the paired measurement holds the entering circuit fixed.

New-family cases: in a separate instrumented run the pass changed 2 of 18 circuits and saved 44 total area units. On 16 unchanged circuits the median pass time was 24 μs and the maximum 0.077 ms. Independent timed portfolio runs can produce different circuits; the paired measurement holds the entering circuit fixed.

Resource limits

The search examines outputs with up to ten relevant inputs on circuits with 2–32 outputs and at most 20,000 gates, subject to memory headroom and a nominal 15 ms allowance. Both solvers passed the three dense official controls at 32, 48 and 64 MiB. The official evaluator reported MEMORY_LIMIT for both on the structured 20-input, 64 MiB control. Run directly under a 64 MiB address-space cap, both passed all 1,048,576 assignments. The official result remains a failure.
