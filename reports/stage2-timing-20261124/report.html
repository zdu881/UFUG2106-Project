Meeting delay targets under an area budget

UFUG2106 · Group 2 · Zeyun DU (50025854) and Jincan Li (50032637) · 24 November 2026

Method

The submitted Stage 1 solver builds Boolean circuits by Shannon decomposition. Stage 2 searches variable orders, shares gates across outputs and rewrites small logic cones. It targets one unit of reserve where feasible. Across the 50 public inputs our engineering objective is a total weighted area below 900,000.

When the selected circuit still exceeds the stated delay, we inspect the truth table of each late output with up to 14 relevant inputs. A Boolean factor can replace a group of input pins if its cofactors reconstruct the output. For less regular tables, we cover equal-valued regions and group identical cofactors into balanced gate trees. For small outputs still above target, the search tries additional balanced partitions of the input pins. The mapper considers both polarities using the input’s gate delays. It prunes temporary gates between outputs and accepts a new circuit only when its maximum delay falls. If timing still falls short, a final cut rewrite works on the reconstructed circuit. These operations share up to 160 ms. The main search uses at most 45% of an explicit time limit, capped at 3.3 s. A public repeat under competing CPU load reached area 910,462 at the former 1.8 s cap. We increased the cap to protect area on the larger cases. The seeded banks have limits of at most 850 ms, so both caps give them identical budgets.
