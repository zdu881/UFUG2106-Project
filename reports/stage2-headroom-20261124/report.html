Circuit area under a delay reserve

UFUG2106 · Group 2 · Zeyun DU (50025854) and Jincan Li (50032637) · 24 November 2026

Design objective

Keep the sum of weighted areas below 900,000 on the 50 released inputs, satisfy each delay target and preserve process time and memory headroom. The program sees one input at a time. Our package-level check sums both public banks after each run. The course grades area, delay and runtime and will also test 50 hidden inputs.

How the method works

The submitted Stage 1 solver used Shannon decomposition and variable-order search. Stage 2 shares gates across outputs, rewrites four-input cuts and factors repeated Boolean functions. The new search aims one integer unit below the stated delay target when the target exceeds one. Among circuits meeting this working target, it prefers smaller area. A separately retained circuit meeting the original target can supply cheaper output cones. An explicit RAM limit disables storage of the second circuit.

For a late path, propagate required arrival times backward. At a late gate, expose up to four frontier signals and evaluate the Boolean function on the frontier. SOP or Shannon mapping can replace that subcircuit with an equivalent, earlier-arriving implementation. Use the change only when the measured whole circuit ranks better. Cut repair and output recovery share a nominal limit of 100 ms and can start only with time left before 72% of a positive process budget.
