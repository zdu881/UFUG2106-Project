This records the earlier portfolio experiment. The current implementation and

## Scope and decision

Retain a bounded direct-mapping portfolio in the experimental Stage 2 source. The previous code allocated zero time to this phase despite a comment suggesting it preserved Stage 1 search. The new phase receives 25% of the time remaining after natural-order mapping. It tries influence, reverse and mutated orders before BDD-size screening. The overall search cap and final delay-excess/area/delay ranking are unchanged. This is not the full Stage 1 search and cannot guarantee per-case non-regression.

The change improves aggregate area on the available Stage 1 set, at the cost of runtime relative to the previous prototype. It remains experimental. Frozen Stage 1, production `src/synth.cpp`, and the six-page Stage 1 report are unchanged. No course endpoint was checked in this session; the 10 November release/deadline observations are historical, not a new status check.
